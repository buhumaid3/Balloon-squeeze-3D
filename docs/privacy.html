🎈 BALLOON SQUEEZE 3D

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: Balloon Squeeze 3D does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There's no account, no login, and no server — the game runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no backend and makes no network requests to collect or transmit data about you or your gameplay.

What's stored on your device

Your progress — levels cleared, shapes discovered, coins, Balloon Pass rank, stats, and achievements — is saved locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not visible to us, to other players, or to any third party
  • Is removed automatically if you delete the app

The in-game "RUNS" leaderboard is local to your device — there is no shared or online leaderboard.

Microphone (optional)

The optional "Blow to Inflate" setting uses your microphone to detect blowing while you hold to inflate a balloon. This is off by default, only active while you're actively using it, processed entirely on your device in real time, and never recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere.

Online multiplayer

An online "VS Real Opponent" mode exists in the game's code but is not available in this version of the app, and does not appear as an option. No multiplayer data is sent or received.

Third parties

Balloon Squeeze 3D does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and does not share data with any third party — because no data is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

Balloon Squeeze 3D does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since the app collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future update adds an online feature — this page will be updated and the date above will reflect the change.
