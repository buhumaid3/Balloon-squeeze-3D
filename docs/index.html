Support & FAQ

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us below.

🎯How do I play?

Hold anywhere on screen to inflate your balloon, and release the moment the gauge is inside the gold zone. Land it clean for a perfect — overshoot and it pops.

What's the gauge on the right?

It shows how full your balloon is. The highlighted band is the gold zone — your release target for that level.

What do wind, drifting, and shrinking zones do?

Later levels add hazards on top of the core hold-and-release mechanic: gusts that push the balloon mid-hold, gold zones that move while you're inflating, and zones that shrink the moment you get close. Some levels are "double-pump" — land the first zone, then immediately pump again for a second.

🗺️Levels, worlds & the roadmap

There are 1,000 levels across 9 rotating worlds — Party Room, Carnival, Beach, City, Disco, Night Sky, and the seasonal Halloween, Winter, and Summer worlds. Tap ROADMAP from the main menu to see your progress and jump into any unlocked level.

🎪Shapes, fusing & the Balloon Pass

How do I unlock new shapes?

Play levels to discover new balloon shapes — 12 base shapes in total. Open SHAPES from the main menu to see what you've found, and once you've discovered at least two, you can fuse them into new hybrid shapes.

What's the Balloon Pass?

A lifetime reward track — you earn XP toward it by playing (including from the Wind Tunnel Boss level), never from a timer or a purchase. There's nothing to buy.

🎙️Blow to Inflate (microphone)

In Settings you can turn on "Blow to Inflate," which lets you blow into your microphone while holding to add extra speed. It's off by default, entirely optional, processed on your device in real time, and never recorded or sent anywhere.

💾Progress & data

Everything — your level progress, shapes, coins, Balloon Pass, stats, and achievements — is saved locally on your device only. There's no account, no login, and no shared leaderboard. If you delete the app or switch devices, that local progress doesn't carry over.

🛠️Troubleshooting

The hold gesture isn't registering.

Make sure you're pressing and holding directly on the screen (not a system gesture area near the very edge), and that no other app is intercepting touch input in the background. Force-quitting and reopening the app usually clears this up.

The game looks frozen or won't load.

Close the app fully (swipe it away from your app switcher) and relaunch. If that doesn't help, restarting your device usually does.

Blow to Inflate isn't picking up my mic.

Check that microphone access is allowed for Balloon Squeeze 3D in your device's Settings → Privacy → Microphone, and that the feature is switched on in the game's own Settings.
